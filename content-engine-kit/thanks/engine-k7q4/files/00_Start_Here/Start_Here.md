## Your setup path (do it in this order)
1. **Read this page and the 7-Day Quick Start** (00). Print the checklist or copy it into your notes.
2. **Open accounts** using the checklist in 09 Tools and Setup. One AI assistant is the only must-have.
3. **Capture your voice** (03). Record or collect your real words first. Everything else depends on it.
4. **Fill your brand kit** (05). Ten to twenty minutes.
5. **Train your AI** (04). Load the instruction block, your voice files, and your brand kit into the AI you use.
6. **Find your creators** (08). Build a list of 10 to 30 creators in and around your niche.
7. **Run outlier research** (08 and 09). Pull their reels, rank them with the 3x rule, keep only the outliers.
8. **Break down the winners with Format DNA** (01). Fill one worksheet per reference reel.
9. **Write your first batch** with the main guide's Workflow 5 and 6, then read every script yourself.
10. **Put the week on your calendar** (06). Import the .ics file and adjust the times.
11. **Check your costs** (07) once a month.

## Rules that keep the content good
- An outlier is a reel with 100K+ views and at least 3x that creator's own median. No numbers, no "proven."
- The hook is what is said and shown in the first 3 seconds. Never the caption.
- Keep the machine (hook, format, rehook, cadence, length, idea, reward). Change the message, angle, and voice.
- At least 3 of your real lines go in the middle of every script.
- You read every script before you film it. The AI drafts; you approve.
- A proven format raises your odds. It does not guarantee views or leads.
