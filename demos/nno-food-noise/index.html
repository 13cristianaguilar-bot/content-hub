Know the noise

Food noise is not hunger.

"Food noise" is the name people use for persistent, intrusive thoughts about food. Hunger is your body asking for fuel. The noise is the craving mind.

Hunger

  • Builds slowly, hours after a meal
  • Any real food sounds good
  • Goes away when you've eaten
  • A body signal

Food noise

  • Shows up right after dinner
  • Wants one specific snack
  • Keeps talking after you're full
  • Gets louder with stress and tired nights
GLP-1

Your body already makes a "fullness hormone."

GLP-1 is a natural hormone released from your gut after you eat. It helps signal that you've had enough. Night Shred GLP-1 contains Sukre, Berberine and Akkermansia to support your body's own GLP-1 production.* Contains no GLP-1. Not a GLP-1 drug. Not a substitute for any prescription medication.
