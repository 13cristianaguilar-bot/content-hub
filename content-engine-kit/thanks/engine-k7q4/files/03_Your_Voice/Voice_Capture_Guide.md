# Capture Your Voice

AI scripts sound like AI because the model never heard you. This module gets your real words into a format your AI can use: a voice intake, a phrase bank, and a do and don't list.

## Step 1: Collect your raw voice (about 60 minutes)
Use what you already have first. Real speech beats anything you write on purpose.
| Source | How to get it | Why it helps |
|---|---|---|
| Voice memo intake | Record yourself answering the Voice Intake Questionnaire (10 to 20 minutes) | Your natural phrasing, stories, and opinions |
| Sales or coaching call transcripts | Export from Zoom, Google Meet, Fathom, or similar | How you explain things to real people |
| Podcasts, lives, webinars | Download and transcribe | Longer stories and signature explanations |
| Posts and captions you wrote yourself | Copy from your profiles | Written tone and favorite phrases |
| Emails and DMs to clients | Copy a few that sound like you | How you give advice one to one |

### How to transcribe for free
- Your phone's built-in voice memo transcription, if it has one
- Whisper (open source) on your computer
- Upload the audio to your AI and ask for a verbatim transcript

Ask for **verbatim** transcripts. Do not let the AI clean up your filler words yet. Some of them are part of your voice.

## Step 2: Build your phrase bank
A phrase bank is a list of lines you actually say. Aim for 30 or more to start.

Paste your transcripts into your AI with this prompt:
```
Below are transcripts of me talking. Pull out 30 to 50 lines that sound most like me:
- phrases I repeat
- the way I explain my main ideas
- short stories or examples I tell
- opinions I state strongly
- how I open and close
Quote them word for word. Do not rewrite or improve them. For each line, note the source and what topic it fits.
```
Then go through the list yourself. Delete anything that does not sound like you. Add lines you know you say that it missed. Save it as your phrase bank (Phrase_Bank_Template.csv).

## Step 3: Write your do and don't list
Fill the Do and Don't template. It tells your AI the edges of your voice: words you use, words you never use, topics you avoid, and how formal you are.

## Step 4: Keep it fresh
- Add new lines every month from new calls, posts, and voice notes.
- When you change a word while filming, add the new version to the bank.
- Mark lines that your audience reacted to (comments, DMs) so the AI uses them more.

## How the voice gets used
In every script, at least 3 phrase-bank lines go in the middle, after the hook and before the call to action. A signature line glued on at the end does not count. See 01 Format DNA, Step 4.
