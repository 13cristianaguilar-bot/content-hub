    Rules

    What the team never does

    1. Never messages a customer. Every quote waits for Steve or Matt.
    2. Never guesses. Missing details become a flag for you.
    3. Never rewrites silently. Every QC change is shown in the quote.
    4. Never rejects a lead. Anything unusual is held for a person.
