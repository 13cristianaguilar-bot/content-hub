Welcome

Play the Connection Game together.

Take turns answering prompt cards. The game cheers on moves that bring you closer and gently beeps when a move slides into the Disconnection Game.

How it works

Connection moves earn points: bids for connection, curiosity, appreciation, and repair attempts.

Disconnection moves trigger the beep: criticism, contempt, defensiveness, and shutting down. You can try a repair or keep going. It is your game.
