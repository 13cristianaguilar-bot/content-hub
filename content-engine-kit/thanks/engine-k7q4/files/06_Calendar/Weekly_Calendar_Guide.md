# Put the Engine on Your Calendar

The system only works if it has time on your calendar. This is the weekly cadence from the main kit, turned into time blocks you can import.

## The weekly blocks (about 3 to 4 hours plus filming)
| Day | Time block | Job | What you use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday | 9:00 to 9:50 AM | Review last week, then research refresh (you + 5 to 15 creators) | Content Hub, outliers.py or Prompt 02, 08 worksheets |
| Tuesday | 9:00 to 10:30 AM | Pick the batch, write scripts with Format DNA, run voice check and QA | 01 Format DNA, main kit Workflow 5 and 6, Prompts 06 to 08 |
| Wednesday | 9:00 to 9:30 AM | Hooks and captions for each approved script | Hook bank, Prompt 05 |
| Thursday | 10:00 to 11:30 AM | Film everything from the teleprompter | 09 teleprompter.html |
| Friday | 9:00 to 9:20 AM | Post, log results, note your edits | Content Hub, your platforms |
| First Monday of the month | 10:00 to 10:45 AM | Update phrase bank, refresh formats, check tool costs | 03, 07 |

The times are a starting point. Move them to fit your week. Keep the order: research, scripts, hooks, film, post.

## Import the calendar file
The file **Content_Engine_Weekly.ics** in this folder holds all six blocks as repeating events. The series starts Monday, November 23, 2026 and repeats every week (the monthly refresh repeats on the first Monday of each month). Times import in your calendar's own time zone.

### Google Calendar (computer)
1. Open calendar.google.com.
2. Optional: make a new calendar for it. Click the plus next to "Other calendars," choose **Create new calendar**, name it "Content Engine."
3. Click the gear icon, then **Settings**, then **Import and export** in the left menu.
4. Under **Import**, click **Select file from your computer** and choose Content_Engine_Weekly.ics.
5. Pick the calendar to add it to (for example "Content Engine"), then click **Import**.
6. Open any event and adjust the time. Choose "This and following events" so the whole series moves.
Google Calendar imports .ics files on a computer, not in the phone app. After import, the events show on your phone.

### Apple Calendar
Double-click the .ics file, choose a calendar, click OK.

### Outlook
Open the .ics file, or use Add calendar, then Upload from file.

## Make the blocks stick
- Treat Thursday filming like a client meeting. Do not move it for small things.
- Put the Content Hub link in each event's description so you open the right file.
- If you miss a week, do not double up. Restart on Monday with the research refresh.
- At the end of each month, check how many scripts you approved, filmed, and posted. That ratio tells you which block needs more time.
