# Meta Muse: Where It Fits in the Content Engine

## What Muse is
Muse is Meta's personal AI agent. In Meta's words, it "can take actions and help get things done with your direction, and can be connected with other apps and services." You log in with your Meta account (Facebook, Instagram, email, or phone number) through the mobile app or at muse.ai.

Muse is in limited testing and is not available in every country yet. Check whether it is available to you before you plan around it. The kit works fully without it.

## What it costs (Meta's help page, read November 16, 2026)
| Plan | Price | Weekly usage |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | Usage limit that refreshes |
| Power | $20 per month | 500M Muse tokens per week |
| Maximum | $100 per month | 3B Muse tokens per week |

Subscriptions renew monthly and can be cancelled any time. You must be 18 or older and in a supported country. Source: https://www.meta.com/help/subscriptions/1021145227643680/

## Where it fits in the workflow
Muse is optional. Think of it as a phone-side assistant next to your main AI (Claude, ChatGPT, Grok Bot, or Cursor), not a replacement for it. Your main AI holds your voice, brand kit, and workflow files. Muse is handy for the moments you are away from your desk.

| Workflow step | How Muse can help | Where the result goes |
|---|---|---|
| Idea capture | Dictate ideas, stories, and lines you just said on a call while they are fresh | Phrase bank and idea list in your main AI project |
| Creator discovery | Brainstorm keywords and creator types for your niche on the go | Keyword and creator worksheets (08) |
| Quick lookups | Ask questions about a topic before you script it | Your script notes, marked [CONFIRM] until you verify |
| Weekly reminders | Use it alongside your calendar to keep the weekly cadence moving | Your calendar (06) |

What Muse can do depends on the features Meta has turned on for your account. Test it on one task before you rely on it.

## Rules when using Muse (or any second AI)
- The outlier numbers still come from real data: your scraper, your manual log, or the creator's actual reels. Do not take view counts from a chat answer.
- The hook still comes from watching the reel. A chat summary is not a media lock.
- Anything factual from a chat goes in as [CONFIRM] until you check it.
- Your main AI project stays the single source of truth for your voice and brand.

## Should you pay for it?
Start on the free plan. Upgrade only if you hit the weekly limit doing real work. In the cost breakdown (07), Muse Power ($20) only appears in the Heavy stack.
