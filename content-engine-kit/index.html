FAQ

Quick answers

Which AI do I need?

Claude, ChatGPT, Grok Bot, or Cursor. Setup guides for each are included. A paid plan helps for weekly batches because of usage limits.

Does my AI scrape Instagram by itself?

No. You pull reels with a scraper on its free tier (Apify or Bright Data), or log them by hand. The kit shows both. Your AI ranks and breaks them down.

What exactly is an outlier?

A reel that beats its own creator's median by 3x or more. Same creator, same audience, so the format is what changed. For hooks you copy, the kit also asks for 100K+ views.

Do I need a big following?

No. You research other creators in your niche. Your follower count does not change the math.

Do I need to code?

No. Copy and paste. outliers.py and the Cursor setup are there if you want them.

How long does it take?

About 60 minutes to set up your AI. The 7-Day Quick Start takes 30 to 60 minutes a day. After that, about 2 to 3 hours a week.

Your own AI plan, and a scraper if you go past its free tier. The tool cost module shows light, medium, and heavy stacks before you spend anything.

What do I get, and in what format?

A 43-page PDF guide, 10 modules as PDF, Word, Markdown, and sheet files, prompts, skill files, templates, a calendar file, outliers.py, and a browser teleprompter. All on your download page right after checkout.

No and no. It is self-serve. You run it, you film. No video editing.
