Paste the system into your AI

One time · about 60 minutes

  • Upload your voice files, brand kit, and the workflow and prompt files.

3

Run your first scan

Day 4 and 5 of the quick start
  • List 10 to 30 creators in your niche.
  • Pull their recent reels with a scraper's free tier or log them by hand. Your AI does not scrape Instagram by itself.
  • Rank them with outliers.py or Prompt 02. Keep reels at 3x or more of their creator's median.
  • Break the winners down with Format DNA, then write 3 scripts.
