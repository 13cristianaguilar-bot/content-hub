**Winner: the event-recap carousel.** The "40 owners / winners" post did **2,054 views — ~2× everything else**, with 13 shares. Shares drove reach: concrete, name-specific proof of what members built travels.

**Runner-up lane: named member-build reels.** Justin's Zoom-clone reel (964 views) and Sagun's content-agent reel both landed. Specific person + specific build + a number ($200K, 3M views, 66 weeks) = the formula.

**Hidden gem: the Sagun reel.** Only 138 reach but a **29.7% engagement rate** — the people who saw it loved it, but it didn't travel. That's a distribution/hook problem, not a content problem. Worth a re-cut with a stronger opening hook.

**Weakest: the POV skit.** Middling on every metric. Fine for personality, but it's not the growth engine.

**Consistent across every top post:** a specific Austin angle, real numbers, and the **"Comment MASTERMIND"** CTA.

### The takeaways
1. **Lead with proof, not theory.** Named wins + numbers > generic education.
2. **Carousels for reach, reels for depth.** Carousels got shared wider; reels drove higher per-view engagement.
3. **Fix hooks on high-engagement / low-reach posts** — the content is good, the first 2 seconds aren't traveling.
4. **Keep the MASTERMIND CTA** on everything driving to the event.
