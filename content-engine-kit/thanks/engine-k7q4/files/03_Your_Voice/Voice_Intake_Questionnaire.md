# Voice Intake Questionnaire

Record yourself answering these out loud, like you are talking to a friend. Do not script it. 10 to 20 minutes is plenty. Then transcribe it word for word.

## About you
1. What do you do, in the way you would say it at a dinner party?
2. Who do you help, and what is going on in their life when they find you?
3. Why did you start doing this? Tell the story.

## Your ideas
4. What is the one thing you wish every client knew before they started?
5. What do most people in your industry get wrong? Why?
6. What advice do you give over and over?
7. What is an opinion you hold that some people disagree with?

## Your stories
8. Tell me about a client or customer moment you are proud of (no names needed).
9. Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned.
10. What is a story you tell on almost every sales call?

## How you talk
11. How do you usually open a conversation with a new client?
12. How do you explain your process in under a minute?
13. Are there words or phrases people say you use a lot?
14. What words or styles make you cringe when you hear them in content?

## Your offer
15. What do you sell, and what happens after someone buys?
16. What do you want people to do after they watch your content?
