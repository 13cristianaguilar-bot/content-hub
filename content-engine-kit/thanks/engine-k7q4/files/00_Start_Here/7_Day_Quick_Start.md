# 7-Day Quick Start Checklist

About 30 to 60 minutes a day. By day 7 you have a ranked list of outliers, your voice loaded into your AI, and your first scripts ready to film.

## Day 1: Accounts and folder
- [ ] Pick your AI: Claude, ChatGPT, Grok Bot, or Cursor (04 Train Your AI)
- [ ] Open the free accounts you need from the checklist in 09 Tools and Setup
- [ ] Make a Google Sheet (or Notion or Airtable) for your Content Hub using the main kit's content-hub-tracker template
- [ ] Copy this whole folder somewhere you keep work files

## Day 2: Your voice
- [ ] Record a 10 to 20 minute voice memo answering the questions in 03 Voice_Intake_Questionnaire
- [ ] Transcribe it (your phone's transcription, Whisper, or your AI)
- [ ] Gather 3 to 5 more sources: call transcripts, posts, emails, captions you wrote yourself
- [ ] Build your phrase bank: 30+ real lines you actually say (03 Phrase_Bank_Template)
- [ ] Fill your Do and Don't list (03)

## Day 3: Brand kit and AI setup
- [ ] Fill the Brand Kit template (05): colors, fonts, tone, pillars, offers, banned words
- [ ] Set up your AI with the instruction block (04)
- [ ] Upload: phrase bank, do and don't list, brand kit, and the main kit's workflow and prompt files
- [ ] Test it: ask for 3 hook options on one of your topics and check they sound like you

## Day 4: Find your creators
- [ ] Write your keyword list (08 Keyword_List_Worksheet)
- [ ] Search Instagram with those keywords and note 10 to 30 creators (08 Creator_List_Worksheet)
- [ ] Mix: same niche, adjacent niches, and 2 to 3 creators outside your niche whose formats you like

## Day 5: Outlier research
- [ ] Pull each creator's recent reels (Apify, Bright Data, or the manual log)
- [ ] Rank them with outliers.py or Prompt 02 in the main kit
- [ ] Keep only reels with 3x+ the creator's median (and 100K+ views for hooks you will copy)
- [ ] Log the top 10 to 20 in your Outlier Worksheet

## Day 6: Break them down
- [ ] Watch each top outlier with sound on
- [ ] Fill one Format DNA worksheet per reel (01): hook, format components, rehook, cadence, length, idea, reward, emotional reward and next step
- [ ] Add every verified on-screen hook to your hook bank

## Day 7: Write and schedule
- [ ] Write 3 scripts with Format DNA (main kit Workflow 5 and Prompt 06)
- [ ] Run the voice check (Prompt 07) and read every script out loud
- [ ] Load the approved scripts into the teleprompter (09)
- [ ] Import the weekly calendar file (06) and book your first filming block

**After day 7:** follow the weekly cadence in 06 Calendar. Compare each post to your own median, not to anyone else's numbers.
